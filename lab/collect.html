Project 2 · Instructor tool

Collect everyone's A/B tests

This page runs entirely in your browser; nothing is uploaded anywhere. It turns students' …_abtest.zip files into the study list for the study page, plus your Qualtrics files.

  1. Download all submissions from Canvas (the single bulk zip is fine; zips inside zips are opened automatically).
  2. Drop them below and check the table. Use Preview to look at any choice.
  3. Click Download upload bundle. In your repo, upload the bundle's lab folder (Add file › Upload files, drag the folder). It replaces lab/studies.js and adds the images. Don't upload instructor_only: it has student names.
  4. The first time: import instructor_only/end_survey.qsf into Qualtrics, then paste that survey's link into QUALTRICS_URL in lab/config.js.
Late submissions: drop all zips again (old and new) and re-upload. Each run rebuilds the full list. If you add or remove studies after data collection has started, change version in lab/config.js, and also re-import the .qsf or add the new fields to your Qualtrics survey.
