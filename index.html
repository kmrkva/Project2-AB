Project 2 Redesign: Aim for a redesign that is…

  • Grounded in a class concept

  • Problem → solution

    What's wrong with the current marketing, and how your change fixes it.

  • Very noticeable

    Visible at a glance, at the size shoppers see it. Tiny wording changes will be missed by survey takers and unlikely to have impact.

  • Likely to have a big impact

    Could plausibly shift ~10% of choices. Our survey sample can't detect small effects.

  • Well-controlled

    Only your concept should change. Image size, crop, price, and other elements (name, ratings, etc.) should stay identical.

  • A fair matchup

    Aim for a brand and competitor for which most people would be on-the-fence or slightly prefer the competitor. If most people have strong preferences for one, your redesign will be unlikely to have a big impact.

  • Realistic

    Looks like something the company could actually use & redesign.

  • Shown as shoppers see it

    Ideally, screenshots of both products will be from the same retail site, in the same format.

Tips for building your After image

  • Any tool works: Photoshop, GIMP, Canva, Figma, PowerPoint, an AI image editor, or Lovable.
  • Start from your Before screenshot. Edit a copy, and put new elements on their own layer.
  • Match the original. Pick colors with the eyedropper; use the same or a look-alike font.
  • Keep the frame fixed. Export at the same pixel size and crop as the Before.
  • Using AI? Ask it to change only one thing and keep everything else exactly the same. Add text yourself afterward.
  • Flip test. View Before and After side by side. Anything changed besides your concept? Fix it.
